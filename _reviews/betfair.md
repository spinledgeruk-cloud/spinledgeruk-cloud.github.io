## Payment methods and deposit limits

Betfair's own support pages list nine deposit methods: debit cards (up to three per account), Apple Pay, Pay by Bank, PayPal, Skrill, Neteller, Rapid Transfer, MuchBetter and Bank Transfer. Most deposits process immediately; bank transfers take 1 to 5 days. Betfair does not publish a single fixed minimum deposit figure across all methods — it states the minimum for your specific method on the deposit screen itself.

## Withdrawal times

Betfair publishes a genuinely useful breakdown by method, which is more transparent than several operators we've reviewed. Pay by Bank withdrawals are described as arriving immediately. Debit card withdrawals have two tiers: an instant option that should land within 1-4 hours (Visa and participating Mastercard), with Betfair's own guidance to allow up to 24 hours before contacting support, and a standard option taking 2-5 working days. Bank holidays do not count towards the working-day total, so factor that in around long weekends.
