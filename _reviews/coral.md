Coral is a trading name of LC International Limited, licensed by the UK Gambling Commission under account number 54743, alongside other Entain-group brands including Ladbrokes, party casino and party poker on the same licence. You can check this directly on the [Gambling Commission's public register](https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/public-register/business/detail/trading-names/54743).

## Payment methods and deposit limits

Coral's published deposit methods for UK customers are Google Pay, Pay by Bank, Apple Pay, Bank Transfer, Maestro, PayPal and paysafecard. Coral's own help pages state plainly that "customers from the UK and Ireland will not be allowed to deposit using credit cards" — Visa and Mastercard debit still work, but credit cards do not, which is standard across UK-licensed operators following the 2020 credit card gambling ban, not specific to Coral. UK customers face a maximum single deposit of £2,000 and are limited to three active payment methods in any rolling 30-day period.

Coral does not publish a fixed minimum deposit figure; instead, the minimum is shown in the cashier at the time you deposit and can vary by method.

## Withdrawal times

We could not find a single published withdrawal-time figure on Coral's own help pages — its help centre directs you to check the timeframe for your specific method inside your account at the time of withdrawal, rather than publishing a standing commitment (for comparison, many operators publish a stated maximum such as "up to 5 business days" — Coral's public help pages do not). If a stated timeframe matters to you, check it inside your own account cashier before depositing, and keep a record of what you were shown if a withdrawal later takes longer than that.

## Our take

Coral's licensing sits under the same Entain umbrella as Ladbrokes, which typically means consistent account-level policies (deposit limits, payment restrictions) across those brands. The absence of a published standing withdrawal-time commitment is the one gap worth knowing about before you deposit — it's not necessarily slower than operators who do publish a figure, but you cannot compare it on that basis without checking your own account first.
