## Verdict in 30 seconds

Two or three sentences. Who this casino suits, who should skip it, and the single fact that decided the rating.

## Licence and ownership

Who operates it, since when, the UKGC account number and what the public register shows (any regulatory sanctions in the last three years, found in the Commission's enforcement reports).

## Registration and verification

What was asked at sign-up, whether ID was required before the first deposit or only before withdrawal, how long verification took.

## Games and providers

Number of slots, live dealer tables, notable providers (Evolution, Pragmatic Play, NetEnt…). RTP information: is it displayed in-game? Any UK stake limit implementation (£5 per spin, £2 for under-25s).

## Bonus terms, read in full

Reproduce the significant terms. Calculate what the wagering means in pounds: a £100 bonus at 10x means £1,000 of bets before withdrawal. Note the max bet, excluded games, expiry, and whether winnings are capped.

## Deposits and withdrawals, tested

Methods available, fees, minimums and maximums. The exact timeline of the withdrawal you made. Whether a pending period exists and whether it can be cancelled (reverse withdrawal is banned in the UK since 2020, so any casino allowing it is a red flag).

## Safer gambling tools

Deposit limits, time-outs, reality checks, self-exclusion, GAMSTOP integration. How easy they are to find.

## Customer support

Channels, hours, and the real question you asked with the response time and quality of the answer.

## Mobile

App or browser, what works and what does not.

## Final rating: X / 10

Rating breakdown in a short table: withdrawals, games, bonus value, support, safer gambling tools.
