LeoVegas Gaming PLC holds UK Gambling Commission account number 39198, registered at a Malta head office, as is standard for operators licensed to serve the UK market from outside it. You can check the licence is current at any time on the [Gambling Commission's public register](https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/public-register/business/detail/premises/39198) — worth doing yourself before depositing anywhere, not just here.

## Payment methods and deposit limits

LeoVegas lists six payment methods for UK players: Visa Debit, Visa Electron, Mastercard, PayPal, Apple Pay and Open Banking. The minimum deposit is £10 through most methods, dropping to £5 if you use Apple Pay. Withdrawal limits vary by method — £5,000 for debit card, £8,000 for Apple Pay, and £35,000 for Open Banking, according to LeoVegas's own payment methods page.

## Withdrawal times

LeoVegas states withdrawals can take "up to five business days" depending on the method chosen, without publishing a per-method breakdown of typical speed. Treat this as the outer bound the operator commits to, not a guaranteed same-day figure — if a withdrawal is taking longer than five business days, that is a basis for a complaint through LeoVegas's own process, and if that goes unresolved, escalation to IBAS (see [our IBAS complaint guide](/guides/ibas-complaint-process-explained/)).

## Welcome offer

New customers depositing and playing £10 receive 50 free spins on Big Bass Splash. The significant terms: the £10 deposit itself carries a 1x wagering requirement, satisfied only by slots, instant win games and Slingo — table games and live casino play do not count towards it. The free spins carry no separate wagering requirement, but expire 3 days after being credited, so they need using promptly. The deposit must be made within 7 days of registering.

This is a comparatively light offer by wagering-requirement standards elsewhere in the market: a 1x requirement on the deposit only, rather than on deposit-plus-bonus, is easier to clear. The tight 3-day expiry on the spins themselves is the term most likely to catch someone out if they sign up and don't play promptly.

## Our take

Based on its published terms, LeoVegas's welcome offer is more straightforward than the multi-stage bonuses common elsewhere in the market, and its Open Banking withdrawal limit (£35,000) is unusually high. The stated 5-business-day withdrawal ceiling is average for the sector rather than a standout — some operators publish faster typical windows for card and e-wallet withdrawals specifically. As with any operator, the terms above reflect what LeoVegas publishes today; check its own site for the current offer before you deposit, since promotional terms change more often than licensing details do.
