Paddy Power Casino is a trading name of PPB Entertainment Limited, which holds active UK Gambling Commission bingo and casino licences under account number 39426, alongside Betfair on the same licence. Both brands sit inside Flutter Entertainment and share the same payments infrastructure, which is why the figures below closely match our [Betfair review](/reviews/betfair/). Check the licence directly on the [Gambling Commission's public register](https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/public-register/business/detail/39426).

## Payment methods and deposit limits

Paddy Power's help centre lists debit cards, Apple Pay, Pay by Bank (Open Banking), Bank Transfer, Neteller, PayPal, Skrill and Rapid Transfer as accepted methods. Its help pages also mention credit cards and prepaid cards in a general listing that appears to cover multiple markets — UK customers should not expect to deposit by credit card, since that has been banned industry-wide for UK gambling since 2020 regardless of what any individual operator's general help page lists. Minimum deposit amounts vary by method and are shown on the deposit screen itself rather than published as one fixed figure.

## Withdrawal times

Paddy Power publishes the same tiered breakdown as Betfair: Pay by Bank withdrawals arrive immediately, an instant card withdrawal option lands within 1-4 hours (allow up to 24 hours before contacting support), and the standard card withdrawal option takes 2-5 working days, excluding bank holidays. If a standard withdrawal hasn't arrived after 5 working days, that is the point at which Paddy Power's own guidance says to contact support.

## Our take

Because Paddy Power and Betfair run on the same Flutter payments infrastructure and share one UKGC licence, the practical experience of depositing and withdrawing is close to identical between the two — the choice between them is more about which app or odds format you prefer than about which pays out faster. The same caveat about the generic credit-card mention on the help pages applies: UK players should rely on the debit and e-wallet methods listed above, not on any credit card option that page mentions for other markets.
