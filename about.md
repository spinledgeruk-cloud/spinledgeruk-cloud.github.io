# About this site

{{ site.title }} is run by {{ site.author_name }}, an independent reviewer based in France writing for players in Great Britain. The site launched in 2026 and is not owned by, or affiliated with, any casino operator.

## What we do

We review online casinos that hold a licence from the UK Gambling Commission. For every review we open an account, deposit real money, play, and request a withdrawal, then report what actually happened: how long the money took to arrive, which documents were requested, what the bonus terms said in full.

## What we do not do

We do not list casinos that are not licensed in Great Britain, whatever they offer to pay us. We do not accept payment for a rating. We do not write about "guaranteed wins", "risk-free" offers or strategies to beat the house, because they do not exist.

## How we make money

Some links on this site are affiliate links. If you open an account through one of them we may receive a commission from the operator. This does not change the price you pay or the rating we give. Full details on the [affiliate disclosure](/affiliate-disclosure/) page.

## Responsible gambling

Gambling carries a risk of harm. We publish [free support resources](/responsible-gambling/) on every page and encourage every reader to set deposit limits before playing.
