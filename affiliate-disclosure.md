# Affiliate disclosure

This page exists so you know exactly how {{ site.title }} is funded before you rely on anything we write.

## We earn commissions from some links

Some links on this site are affiliate links. When you click one and open an account with the operator, the operator may pay us a commission, either a one-off amount or a share of their revenue from your account. You pay nothing extra: the price of a deposit, a withdrawal or a bet is identical whether you arrive through our link or not.

## What this does not change

Ratings, verdicts and rankings are decided before any commercial conversation and are not adjusted for commission levels. A casino that pays us more does not rank higher. A casino we have no partnership with can be reviewed and rated exactly the same way. If we stopped being paid by an operator, its review would stay online unchanged.

## What we will never do

We will not list an operator that is not licensed by the UK Gambling Commission. We will not describe any offer as "free", "risk-free" or "guaranteed" unless it genuinely carries no risk to your money, which in practice is never the case. We will not hide bonus terms: wagering requirements, expiry dates, maximum bets and game restrictions are shown next to every offer we mention.

## How we test

Each review is based on a real account, a real deposit and a real withdrawal request made by the author. Withdrawal times are measured from the moment the request is submitted to the moment funds are visible. Where an operator changes its terms after publication, we update the page and show the update date.

## Identifying sponsored content

Where a page or a section is paid for in a way that goes beyond the standard affiliate link (for example a sponsored placement), it is marked **Ad** at the top of the page, in line with the UK CAP Code and the Advertising Standards Authority's rules on affiliate marketing. At the date of this page no such content exists on the site.
