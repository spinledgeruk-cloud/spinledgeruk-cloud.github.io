# Contact

Email: <a href="mailto:{{ site.contact_email }}">{{ site.contact_email }}</a>

We reply within three working days. Please tell us which page you are writing about.

**Corrections.** If a fact in a review is out of date, say so and we will re-test and update the page, with the update date shown at the top.

**Complaints about a casino.** We cannot resolve disputes with an operator. Contact the operator's support first; if unresolved after eight weeks, you can escalate to the operator's named Alternative Dispute Resolution provider (listed in their terms) or to the [Independent Betting Adjudication Service](https://www.ibas-uk.com).

**Gambling harm.** If gambling is causing you problems, please do not wait to write to us. Free, confidential help is available now from the [National Gambling Helpline on 0808 8020 133](https://www.begambleaware.org).

**Operators and affiliate managers.** We accept partnership enquiries from UKGC-licensed operators only. Ratings are not for sale.
