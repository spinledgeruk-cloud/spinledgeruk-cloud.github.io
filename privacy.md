# Privacy policy

## Who we are

## What we collect

This is a static website hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not run accounts, newsletters, comments or tracking cookies of our own.

**Hosting logs.** GitHub Pages, our hosting provider, may collect IP addresses and standard request data in its server logs for security purposes. Their policy is at [docs.github.com/site-policy](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

**Analytics.** If we add analytics, this page will be updated to name the provider and the data collected before it goes live.

**Emails you send us.** If you contact us by email we keep your message for as long as needed to answer it and for up to 12 months afterwards, then delete it.

**Affiliate links.** When you click an affiliate link, the operator's affiliate platform sets a cookie on the operator's domain to attribute your registration to this site. That cookie is governed by the operator's privacy policy, not ours. We receive aggregated statistics only (clicks, registrations, commission); we never receive your name, contact details or gambling activity.

## Legal basis

Where processing occurs it is based on our legitimate interest in running and securing the site (UK GDPR Article 6(1)(f)), or on your consent where you contact us.

## Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask us what personal data we hold about you, ask for it to be corrected or deleted, and object to processing. Email us and we will respond within one month. You can also complain to the [Information Commissioner's Office](https://ico.org.uk).

## Children

This site is for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

## Changes

We will update this page whenever our data practices change and show the date of the last update at the top.
