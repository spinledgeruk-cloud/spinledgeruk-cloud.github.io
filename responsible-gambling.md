# Responsible gambling

Online gambling is entertainment that costs money. Over time the house always wins; that is how every casino game is built. Nothing on this site changes that, and no bonus, system or strategy does either.

## Get help now

If gambling is affecting you, your finances or the people around you, these services are free, confidential and open 24 hours a day.

- **National Gambling Helpline:** [0808 8020 133](tel:08088020133), run by GamCare, phone and live chat.
- **[BeGambleAware](https://www.begambleaware.org):** advice, self-assessment and links to treatment.
- **[GamCare](https://www.gamcare.org.uk):** free counselling and support groups across the UK.
- **[Gamblers Anonymous UK](https://www.gamblersanonymous.org.uk):** peer support meetings.
- **[NHS Gambling Services](https://www.nhs.uk/nhs-services/mental-health-services/nhs-gambling-services/):** specialist clinics, free through the NHS.

## Block yourself from every UK site

[GAMSTOP](https://www.gamstop.co.uk) is a free national self-exclusion scheme. One registration blocks you from every online gambling operator licensed in Great Britain for 6 months, 1 year or 5 years, and cannot be undone early. Every casino reviewed on this site is covered by it.

You can also block gambling transactions at the bank level: most UK banks (Monzo, Starling, Barclays, HSBC, Lloyds, NatWest, Santander and others) offer a gambling block in their app, and [Gamban](https://gamban.com) blocks gambling sites and apps on your devices.

## Before you play

Decide the amount you can afford to lose entirely, and set it as a deposit limit inside the casino account before your first deposit. UK-licensed casinos are required to offer deposit limits, session reminders, time-outs and self-exclusion in the account settings. Use them at the start, not after a bad night.

Never gamble to win back money you have lost. Never gamble with borrowed money or money meant for bills. Never gamble when upset, tired or drinking.

## Signs to watch for

Spending more than planned, hiding gambling from others, borrowing to gamble, feeling restless or irritable when not gambling, or gambling to escape problems. If any of these sound familiar, the helpline above is the right next call; you do not need to be in crisis to use it.

## Under 18

It is illegal for anyone under 18 to gamble online in Great Britain, and this site is for adults only. Parents can use free filtering software such as [Net Nanny](https://www.netnanny.com) or built-in device parental controls to block gambling content.
